Yamamoto card image Showcase a Japanese baseball card while supporting the story about future MLB stars. The photograph and caption appear in normal document flow. CSS float and shape-outside allow text to wrap around the image on larger screens. Border-radius softens the image corners. The article section contains the floated figure using display: flow-root. No additional stacking context is needed. Below 52rem, the figure returns to normal flow. If shape-outside is unsupported, the image and text remain readable. Test at 320px, 768px, and 1280px, plus 200% zoom. Verify wrapping, caption readability, and fallback behavior.
NPB Collector's Feature badge Identify the editorial feature and add a visual detail to the header. The badge appears after the introduction in normal document flow. Relative positioning on the header and absolute positioning on the badge keep it anchored in the upper-right corner. The feature-header is the containing block because it uses position: relative. No additional stacking context is required. At screen widths of 52rem or smaller, the badge uses position: static and returns to normal flow below the introduction. Test at 320px, 768px, and 1280px. Check that the badge does not overlap the headline at 200% zoom or when the title is longer.
In This Story sidebar Help readers navigate between the three main sections of the article. The navigation appears before the article content in normal document flow. CSS position: sticky keeps the navigation visible while scrolling at desktop widths. The sidebar is a grid item inside the .story-layout container. This container bounds the sticky sidebar so it stops before the documentation section. The --layer-sticky variable controls its z-index. Below 52rem, the sidebar remains in normal flow. Browsers without sticky support display regular navigation. Test at 320px, 768px, and 1280px, plus 200% zoom. Confirm the sidebar stays visible without covering article content.
Editorial pull quote Highlight an important statement about Japanese baseball card collecting. The quotation appears in normal document flow between two paragraphs. Relative positioning, a negative margin, and z-index create a controlled overlap into the article gutter. A shadow adds visual depth. The quote creates its own stacking context through position: relative and z-index: 10. It remains within the article section. Below 52rem, the negative margin and raised positioning are removed, allowing the quote to return to its normal layout. Test at 320px, 768px, and 1280px. Verify no overlap with navigation, text, or keyboard focus at 200% zoom.
Article stacking context and layer system Keep the article's decorative elements organized without interfering with navigation or other content. All article content remains in normal document flow and follows the HTML source order. CSS isolation: isolate creates a stacking context for the article. A tokenized layer scale uses base (0), raised (10), sticky (20), and overlay (30). The overlay token is reserved for future use. The article creates its own stacking context. The pull quote uses the raised layer inside it, while the sticky sidebar uses the sticky layer outside it. Without isolation support, the article remains readable. The layout does not depend on overlapping content for its meaning. Inspect stacking contexts in browser developer tools. Test at 320px, 768px, and 1280px, plus 200% zoom. Verify links remain clickable and focus indicators remain visible.
Rui Muneyama feature photograph Introduce Japanese baseball card designs through an original collectible card photograph. The photograph and caption appear together in a figure within normal document flow. CSS clip-path creates symmetrical angled corners for a modern editorial appearance. Border-radius rounds the surrounding figure container. The figure remains inside the article's stacking context. Its relative positioning does not create an additional stacking context. If clip-path is unsupported or disabled, the complete rectangular photograph remains visible with its caption. Test at 320px, 768px, 1280px, and 200% zoom. Disable clip-path in Firefox Developer Tools to verify the fallback.
